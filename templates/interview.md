# Interview guide (loose idea → superprompt)

Rules for the agent: **one question at a time**; for each, give your recommendation and why
("I would choose X because Y; does that work?"). Skip what the person has already answered. If an answer
raises a bigger question, dig deeper before moving on. At the end, show the superprompt and ask for approval.

## Block 1: problem and audience

1. What everyday situation made you think of this app? Tell me about a real case.
2. Who feels this pain? Describe a specific person (job, age, phone they use).
3. How does this person solve it today? (paper, WhatsApp, spreadsheet, another app)
4. What is wrong with the current approach? What do they lose (time, money, customers)?

## Block 2: product

5. If the app did **just one thing** very well, what would it be? (becomes J01)
6. What is the second? (becomes J02; both form the vertical slice)
7. Does it need to work without internet? Where is the person when using it?
8. Does it hold other people's data (customers, students, patients)? Does it need an account?
9. What must the app **never** do or promise?

## Block 3: business

10. Who pays? The same person who uses it?
11. Subscription, ads, both, or neither (portfolio app, free)?
12. What is free and what is paid? What makes someone subscribe?
13. How much do you think they would pay per month? (becomes a hypothesis)
14. Do you know competitors? Which ones? What are they missing?

## Block 4: constraints and visuals

15. Android, iPhone or both at launch? Is your Play account personal or an organization account?
16. Is there a deadline? Monthly server budget?
17. Do you already have a name, colors, logo? Any app whose look you admire?
18. Which AI tools do you have? (decides the working mode)

## Warning signs (the agent must point these out)

| Sign | Follow-up question |
| --- | --- |
| "For everyone" | Who is the first person who will use it during launch week? |
| MVP with 15 features | Which 2 prove the idea works? |
| "We will figure out revenue later" | How many users does the app need to pay for itself? |
| Marketplace (two sides) | Which side arrives first and why does it stay without the other? Liquidity is the risk |
| Feature the store prohibits (own payment for digital content) | Show the rule and alternative |
| Sensitive data (health, minors, financial) | Explain the compliance cost first |
